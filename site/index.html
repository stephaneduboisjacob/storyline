Questions

Everything families ask

Does she need internet or a smartphone?

No. She only needs the phone she already has — landline or simple mobile. We call her; she answers. That's the whole interface.

In which languages does it work?

French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, Russian and Chinese — including natural code-switching. If she speaks franglais, so do we.

Who consents to all this?

She does. Being called, being recorded, who may listen, and what may happen one day — everything is decided by her, recorded in her own voice, changeable any week. The family can never override her choices.

What happens to her recordings?

They are encrypted at rest and belong to the family. Export everything, any time, in standard formats — forever, even if you cancel. Deletion is real: crypto-shredded on request.

What does it cost?

$15/month for the weekly calls, private podcast and family library. The annual hardcover book is $89. Card or Bitcoin. Cancel anytime — the archive stays yours.

Is this a medical or emergency service?

No. It's a storyteller with very good manners. If she ever sounds unwell, we tell the family gently — but this is company, not monitoring.
